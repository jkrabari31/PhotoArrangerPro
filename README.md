# Passport & Photo Print Sheet Arranger Pro (Portable Edition)
**made by : Jaimin Rabari Mo.9574782643**

A modern, professional desktop application to interactively crop, customize borders/colors, adjust spacing/gaps between photos for easy scissor cutting, and arrange photos onto **selectable paper formats** (**4×6″, 8×12″, 8×10″, 5×7″, and A4**).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. ☀️ Clean Dedicated Light Mode & Header Attribution
- Permanently styled in a modern, crisp **Light Mode**.
- Prominent developer attribution displayed in bold at the center of the top header:
  **`made by : Jaimin Rabari Mo.9574782643`**

### 2. 📄 Multi-Paper Size Layout Engine
Select your desired paper format from the **Paper Layout** dropdown:
- **`4 × 6 inch (8 Photos)`** *(Default)*: 4 columns × 2 rows on standard 4R photo paper.
- **`8 × 12 inch (32 Photos)`**: 8 columns × 4 rows on studio 8R/S8R paper ($3600 \times 2400\text{ px}$ @ 300 DPI).
- **`8 × 10 inch (20 Photos)`**: 5 columns × 4 rows ($3000 \times 2400\text{ px}$ @ 300 DPI).
- **`5 × 7 inch (8 Photos)`**: 4 columns × 2 rows with generous cutting margins ($2100 \times 1500\text{ px}$ @ 300 DPI).
- **`A4 Sheet (28 Photos)`**: 7 columns × 4 rows on standard international document paper ($3507 \times 2481\text{ px}$ @ 300 DPI).

### 3. ✂️ Inter-Photo Spacing & Scissor Corridors
- **Space Between Photos (Gap Slider):** Adjust spacing from **`0.0 mm` up to `6.0 mm`** (in `0.5 mm` steps).
- **Outer Edge Margin Slider:** Add **`0.0 mm` to `6.0 mm`** around the sheet perimeter to ensure no printer bleed cuts into your photos.
- **Quick Spacing Presets:**
  - `0mm (Tight)`: Edge-to-edge layout.
  - `2mm (Ideal)`: Recommended comfort margin for easy cutting with scissors without clipping into adjacent photos.
  - `4mm (Wide)`: Generous cutting space.
- **Scissor Cut Corridors:** Dashed scissor guide lines run down the exact center of each gap, so cutting along the line gives each photo a clean, uniform margin.
- **Live Physical Dimension Badge:** Displays exact calculated dimensions in real time (e.g., `Photo: 1.40″ × 1.87″ (35.6 × 47.5 mm)`).

### 4. 🖱️ Interactive Custom Cropping Studio (Stage 1)
- **Direct Visual Cropping:** Drag the selection box to position freely over the face/photo.
- **Corner Handles:** Drag the corners (NW, NE, SW, SE) to resize the crop area.
- **Locked Aspect Ratio:** Automatically locked to **1.5 : 2.0 (3:4)** to match standard passport and ID proportions.
- **Composition Aids:** Rule-of-thirds alignment guide lines inside the box and dimmed background masking.
- **Rotation:** Quick `↺ 90° Left` and `↻ 90° Right` rotation buttons for photos taken sideways on smartphones.

### 5. 🎨 Complete Border & Color Control
- **Border Toggle:** Enable or disable photo borders with a single switch.
- **Border Width Slider:** Adjust thickness smoothly from **1 px to 20 px**.
- **Color Picker:** Click the border color swatch to open the full Windows RGB Color Dialog.
- **Quick Palette Swatches:** One-click presets for **White, Off-White, Light Gray, Slate, Black, Navy Blue, Red, and Gold**.

### 6. 🖼️ Real-Time Sheet Print Preview (Stage 2)
- **Live Updating:** As you drag or resize the crop box or switch paper sizes, the full sheet preview updates immediately.
- **Modern Display:** Styled with soft drop shadow against a clean light canvas.

### 7. 🖨️ High-Definition Print Output
- **300 DPI (HD)** or **600 DPI (Ultra HD)**.
- Embeds exact DPI metadata and saves at 98% JPEG quality with no chroma blur.

## 📂 Quick Start

1. Double-click **`PhotoSheetArranger_4x6.exe`** in this folder.
2. Click **"📂 Choose Photo..."** and select your photo.
3. Drag the crop box over the face and drag corners to resize.
4. Select your **Paper Layout** (`4 × 6 inch`, `8 × 12 inch`, etc.).
5. Adjust **"Space Between Photos"** to your preferred cutting distance (e.g. `2.0 mm`).
6. Choose your **Border Width** and **Border Color**.
7. Click **"💾 Export Sheet (JPG)"**.
